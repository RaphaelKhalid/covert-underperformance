## Third-party task data
Rollouts quote task text from these datasets, at the revisions pinned in `spec.json`:

- MBPP, Austin et al. 2021, Google Research, CC-BY-4.0. Task text has been edited: names and bugs were changed.
- HumanEval, Chen et al. 2021, OpenAI, MIT.
- APPS, Hendrycks et al. 2021, through `codeparrot/apps`, listed as MIT. Its problems were gathered from sites such as Codeforces, AtCoder, Codewars and Kattis.
